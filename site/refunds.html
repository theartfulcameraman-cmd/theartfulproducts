Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Most of what we make is personalised, so here's exactly how cancellations, reprints and refunds work.

Cancelling an order

  • Ready-made items: you can cancel any time before the order is dispatched for a full refund.
  • Personalised items: you can cancel for a full refund until you approve the digital preview. Once you've approved it, printing starts and the order can't be cancelled.
  • Bulk and event orders: cancellation terms are set out in your quote.

To cancel, message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number. [Confirm or change these cancellation windows]

Returns

Because personalised pieces are made for you, they can't be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged or with a mistake on our side. Ready-made items can be returned unused, in original packaging, within 7 days of delivery; return shipping is paid by the customer.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your piece arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or doesn't match the preview you approved, send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We'll reprint and resend it free of charge, or refund you in full if you prefer.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet) through Razorpay. Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of approval; your bank may take a few more days to show it.
