Where we ship

When your order ships

  • Ready-made items (magnets, keychains, medal holders): dispatched within 3–5 working days.
  • Personalised items (route keepsakes and frames): dispatched within 7–10 working days after you approve the digital preview we send you.
  • Bulk and event orders: lead time is agreed in your quote.

Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes 2–5 working days for metro cities and 4–8 working days elsewhere. We ship with [courier / shipping partner], and you'll get a tracking link by WhatsApp or email when your order ships.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,799 or more. Smaller orders have a flat shipping charge of ₹79. Digital gift cards have no shipping charge.

Damaged or missing parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, please share a photo or unboxing video within 48 hours of delivery and we'll reprint and resend your piece at no cost. If tracking shows delivered but you haven't received it, contact us within 7 days and we'll raise it with the courier.

Wrong address

Please check your address and PIN code carefully at checkout. If a parcel is returned because of an incorrect address or failed delivery attempts, we can re-ship it once you pay the shipping charge again.
